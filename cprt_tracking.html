Using today’s sitemap to call next quarter

Copart’s inventory today is the pool that sells over the coming weeks, so a reading now should predict the next quarter’s units — Copart says so itself: “year-over-year changes in inventory levels can be a directional indicator of prospective unit sales.” The data agrees: on insurance units the one-quarter-lead alignment lifts R² from 0.52 to 0.83.

Read the effective sample first. 13 historical pairs, but only 6 distinct reported quarters — several share an endpoint, so they are not independent. Treat n as ~6. The confidence intervals below are wide for that reason and are the honest headline, not the point estimate.

QA rules

  • Cross-page overlap ≤ 5%. lot.xml paginates at 50,000 URLs and each page rebuilds on its own schedule; when they fall out of sync the boundary shifts and lots appear on two pages. Yesterday’s capture hit 14.8% overlap and was rejected; today’s is 0.1%.
  • Distinct (union) count, never the sum of pages.
  • Complete page set required — non-negotiable. Dropping it lifts n from 13 to 40 but collapses r from 0.91 to 0.17, because truncated subsets get compared against full counts.
  • YoY window 300–430 days, matched to the nearest fiscal quarter-end within 45 days, so seasonality cancels.
  • 12 of 27 archived captures excluded by these rules.

Limits. Coverage ratio is unknown — Little’s Law implies 150–310k true inventory against ~140–190k listed, so this is a sample of unpublished size. Right-censored at 200,000 (4 pages × 50,000).
