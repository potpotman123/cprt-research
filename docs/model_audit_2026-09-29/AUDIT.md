# Independent revenue-model audit — 29 September 2026

## Decision

**Architecture: coherent for conditional revenue scenarios. Calculations: passed the checks described below. Forecast evidence: insufficient for an unqualified sign-off. Fable: ready to build a transparent scenario workbook, with the unresolved inputs visible.**

This review adopts an outside portfolio-manager perspective but is an internal analytical review, not third-party assurance. Scope is the revenue projection only, following the user's clarification. Earnings, cash flow, WACC and valuation are deliberately outside scope and are not deficiencies in this audit.

The existing model is more developed than a simple units × fee spreadsheet. It handles shared claim selection, expected versus realized salvage, fee bands, service-event accounting, benchmark perimeters and guarded timing interfaces. I found no material arithmetic error in the default/reference and saved thesis scenarios. The principal risk is using an elaborate conditional mechanism as if its response coefficients were measured.

## Most important questions before treating it as a forecast

| Priority | Finding and why it matters | Evidence needed / treatment for Fable |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **The two causal links that generate the short are unmeasured.** Aggregate aftermarket growth does not identify recycled displacement within the same component. Recycler contribution, bid response and influence on marginal auction prices are assumptions. The larger case's revenue delta falls from $90.66m to $27.38m when recycler transmission falls from 50% to 10%; RPU then rises slightly. | Show separate OEM→aftermarket and recycled→aftermarket quantities, price discounts, exposed donor contribution and bid transmission. Mark assumed. Obtain compatible component/repair and recycler records before adopting a central case. Buyer nationality or winning share cannot identify marginal price influence. |
| 1 | **Calibration matches levels, not the total-loss response.** Six repair medians, four vehicle-value scales and two dispersions match twelve targets. All twelve are now training targets. There is no independent estimate of the probability mass close to the repair/total boundary. | Display the six TLFs, four selected values and two repaired means as calibration, with a separate validation panel. Retain 0%/50%/100% response sensitivities; these are structural cases, not statistical confidence intervals. An all-disposition cost-gap histogram would be more useful than further flexible fitting. |
| 1 | **The exposed population is broader than the evidence.** The same source/repair/price multipliers are applied across US insurance cohorts. Eligibility for substitutable collision parts, component overlap, age/model availability, insurer rules and different loss causes are not measured. Cheap interchangeable panels cannot automatically represent every repair or donor. | Add an explicit applicability layer before a point forecast: eligible component share, claim/cohort share, donor contribution and geography. The existing “threshold-responsive fraction” tests sensitivity; it is not measured component eligibility. Combined CAT/other-claim treatment remains a declared approximation. |
| 1 | **Historical reconciliation does not identify insurance units or revenue composition.** Each prior-year quarter is anchored to its reported service dollars, with 90% of US service revenue assigned to insurance and assumed fees used to infer units. This is a legitimate normalization, not an independently reconstructed operating history. | Keep reported totals separate from inferred insurance units and core/title/delivery splits. At 80% insurance allocation, the larger thesis delta is $80.58m, versus $90.66m at 90%. Carry both; do not label the bounds observed. |
| 1 | **The reference is a scenario, not a completed operating forecast.** It continues +3.7% insurance ASP growth and international Q4 economics, holds repair/value/claims drivers neutral except fleet roll, repeats same-quarter carrier conditions and holds other US flat. International continuation contributes about **$89.91m** annual service revenue—nearly the larger aftermarket headwind. | Show the full reference driver sheet and separately source/update each branch. A thesis delta is not a forecast miss. “Neutral” carrier allocation is a comparison convention, not a verified continuation of the latest customer state. |
| 2 | **Historical populations do not perfectly match.** Annual all-loss age TLFs/repair costs are combined with through-October non-comprehensive total-loss values and inferred claim weights. Fleet births combine calendar-year sales with EPA model-year production composition; survival/claims weights are proxies. | Preserve population/period labels and the CCC age-bucket reporting disclaimer. No exact aggregate reconciliation claim. The same assumption can contaminate both calibration and apparent validation. |
| 2 | **RPU depends on unobserved economic composition.** A selected current posted buyer schedule is applied to historical/forecast populations; buyer mix, seller terms, fixed fees, ancillary adoption and gross/net choices are partly assumed. Damage and recovery have an imposed rank relationship. | Keep buyer schedules, mix, seller charges, title/delivery and selected-value changes separate. Test fee/mix/selection effects without explaining the residual as measured service adoption. A model-average RPU decline is not necessarily like-for-like fee deflation. |
| 2 | **The timing and catalyst are unspecified.** Main results are full-year uniform shocks with sale-equivalent timing and, in the main case, immediate expected-salvage updating. No actual adoption ramp, opening backlog or recognition lag identifies the affected quarter. | Label full-year comparative statics. Retain incremental-delay and no-expectation-update cases; do not promise a near-term miss. Physical timing remains disabled until opening cohorts, lags and vintage economics are provided. |
| 2 | **Total-company scope and benchmarks remain gated.** Legacy service revenue is the strongest-defined output. Purchased revenue has a coarse comparison overlay; acquired service classification and eliminations remain unavailable. JPM September 11 is a dated ex-ACV service benchmark; CapIQ is total revenue with unresolved perimeter. | Separate legacy services, purchased sales and acquired revenue. Preserve nulls for unavailable consolidated totals. If Fable presents total-company revenue, these branches need completion; if it presents legacy services, clearly title that perimeter. |
| 2 | **New adoption must be incremental to the baseline.** The observed 2024–25 source-share changes predate the FY26 revenue anchors. Reapplying them as fresh FY27 shocks double-counts history. Aftermarket also competes with OEM, supports rebuilders and can be offset by donor scarcity. | Model explicit future source transitions versus the reference. Include a countercase. Do not infer a downward spiral or outperformance versus expectations from the historical sourcing trend alone. |

Additional economic omissions to keep visible: net salvage costs beyond the assumed seller charge; operational/administrative overrides of a pure economic total-loss threshold; part availability, returns and labor/fitment costs; recycled pricing/inventory adjustment; foreign/end-use demand; and potential auction/buyer-mix responses to lower supply. These do not require adding ten speculative submodels now. They define where a simpler explicit range is more honest than an apparently precise coefficient.

## What passed, and what that means

- **43 existing core checks and 28 aftermarket checks passed again.** They cover accounting identities, selection/price separation, response counterexamples, historical-base preservation, service bundling, timing conservation and acquisition/FX gates.
- **42 additional audit checks passed.** These are not 42 independent empirical validations. They include all 32 live quarterly geography × revenue controls against their four local SEC exhibits; annual service totals; fiscal weights; all six age-TLF targets against the source CSV; independent recomputation of saved calibration moments; birth conservation, fleet-cell coverage, carrier input validity, source fingerprints and fee-grid identity.
- **All twelve calibration targets reproduce**, without rerunning the fitting procedure: six probabilities, four selected values and two repaired means. Two original CCC chart images were visually checked. The earlier selected-value “holdout” became a calibration input; its filename does not restore independence.
- **All 15 core and 11 aftermarket scenarios were regenerated.** The four key aftermarket output files matched their pre-audit hashes exactly. Core tables reproduced with a maximum absolute numeric difference of 4.66e-10 across mixed-unit fields, consistent with floating-point evaluation differences; text/shape did not change. No economic conclusion changed.
- **Source checks are scoped.** The carrier workbook and prior extracted input snapshot match saved hashes; workbook formulas were not independently recomputed. Every live input family was traced, but this is not an exhaustive new verification of every upstream raw observation, source methodology or archived experiment. No missing empirical coefficient becomes validated because its source file exists.

An earlier independent historical reconstruction missed US quarterly revenue by approximately $47.88m, $89.89m and $19.21m before its Q4 anchor. That version is superseded operationally; those errors are **not current-engine errors**, but they remain evidence that accounting reanchoring did not establish predictive validity. The saved joint-selection diagnostic had zero accepted cases under its tighter tolerance; this audit did not rerun it or rebrand looser cases as validation.

No paid collection, OCR, scraping, outreach, agents or new fitting occurred. Source freshness is not an independent causal holdout.

Further work should prioritize a compatible claim threshold distribution and matched recycled-component economics. The audit stops short of expensive collection or more weak proxies. See `FABLE_HANDOFF.md` for the exact implementation contract, files and acceptance checks.
