# Repair-cost source map and bounded discovery results

## Objective and ranking logic
Target: compare insurer-allowed amounts for specified collision-repair operations across cars, SUVs and pickups, with model year, equipment, repair date/region and estimate stage controlled. A professional estimate, engineering requirement, shop bill and insurer payment are distinct evidence classes. No source is called insurer-approved without explicit support.

Rank below is practical research priority under current access, not the intrinsic value of a proprietary dataset. Insurer/shopping-workflow records could be the gold standard but are harder to obtain. Rankings are qualitative judgments based on relevance, accessibility, comparability, bias and effort; no invented numerical access probabilities.

## Concrete findings (limited extracts)

**AAA, December 2023:** four hypothetical repair scenarios for 2023 Camry, Rogue and F-150; professional insurance estimators in Michigan and Southern California used CCC ONE. Common body/mechanical rates were $75/$110 per hour. Front-scenario mean total estimate was $11,708.29, including $1,540.92 attributed to ADAS. This is scenario evidence, not paid claims or a segment premium. Vehicle-level chart values were not reliably extracted this pass. [Report, printed pp. 7, 13–14](https://newsroom.aaa.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/11/Report_Cost-of-ADAS-Repairs-FINAL-23.pdf).

**CRASH Network/Collision Advice, January 2024:** 717 facilities across 48 states responded. For raw-plastic preparation, Progressive DRP responses: 61.0% always and 20.3% most; GEICO DRP: 27.8% and 30.6%. Reported response counts are 62 and 37, with never-asked shares 4.8% and 2.7%. Payment-frequency percentages exclude never-asked respondents. These are shop reports, not claim-weighted approval probabilities. Selection and small subgroups prevent causal insurer comparisons. [Original report hosted by repairer association, pp. 7–8, 14](https://ohioautobody.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/2024-Who-Pays-for-What-Refinish.pdf).

**CCC 2026:** reports 2025 non-comprehensive repairable averages of 13.0 replaced parts and 26.7 labor hours. Its age segmentation and supplement statistics can constrain model totals, but do not supply the desired body × age × damage × operation cross-tab. [Report](https://www.cccis.com/reports/crash-course-2026).

**Mitchell Q2 2026:** publishes repairable severity by powertrain; US BEV $5,684 and ICE $4,955. This demonstrates available segmentation, not a car/SUV comparison and not a causal technology premium. [Report index](https://www.mitchell.com/news-insights/plugged-in).

**DEG:** public inquiries address missing labor, parts and included operations. Use to avoid comparing differently defined work. [Method](https://degweb.org/inquiry-process/).

**S&P:** vehicle-data product supports age/model/segment/geography filters. Useful population weights; no claim of current subscription access. [Product](https://www.spglobal.com/mobility/en/products/automotive-market-data-analysis.html).

**Thatcham/GDV:** repairability and risk assessments exist; GDV describes examining parts prices for 20 vehicle types through Audatex. These do not establish US allowed costs. [Thatcham](https://www.thatcham.org/vehicle-risk-rating/), [GDV](https://www.gdv.de/gdv/medien/medieninformationen/preise-fuer-auto-ersatzteile-steigen-weiter-36438).

Other verified leads: [HLDI](https://www.iihs.org/research-areas/auto-insurance/insurance-losses-by-make-and-model), [CCC estimating databases](https://help.cccis.com/webhelp/repair_facility/estimating/web/Content/Estimating/Databases/Overview%20Databases.htm), [Boyd report](https://s25.q4cdn.com/123825503/files/doc_financials/2025/ar/2025-Annual-Report.pdf), [Solera appraisal service](https://www.solera.com/blog/xpertestimate-auto-claims-solutions/).

## End-to-end comparison pipeline
1. Define common work before observing price: one component replacement, specified ancillary work and equipment, same estimate system/version where possible. Keep scope-controlled cost separate from scenario damage severity.
2. Seed scenarios from a structured source; retain vehicle-specific required differences. Newer-model studies can test architecture but cannot silently calibrate older fleet cohorts.
3. Record hours, labor category, parts/source, paint/materials, scans, calibration and overlap deductions. Use OEM/DEG definitions to avoid double counting removal/reinstallation already included in replacement.
4. Preserve original amounts and show common-rate normalization separately. Missing lines remain unknown rather than zero.
5. Label reimbursement status. Use survey evidence to identify uncertain reimbursement, never multiply cost by the fraction of shops answering always/most as though it were a per-claim probability.
6. Produce operation-level ratios only for comparable scope; keep price dates and equipment explicit. Require multiple models before interpreting a body-class effect.
7. Add damage/operation frequency only from compatible data. Aggregate CCC constraints cannot identify unobserved segment weights. Scenario results remain scenarios until frequency evidence exists.
8. Link to total-loss economics only after considering ACV, salvage recovery and repair-selection bias. Repaired-vehicle averages do not identify the unobserved repair cost of totaled vehicles.

## Execution decision and resource gate
This pass used 18 web queries in five batches and targeted source reads. No agents, bulk OCR, paid access, outreach or full 75-page survey extraction. AAA direct download returned 403; web text was readable, but chart images were not available as verified numerical observations. Current survey download route requests contact information; no information was submitted. A public 2024 association-hosted copy supplied the limited extract above.

Next bounded priority: recover and visually verify only the AAA vehicle-level result charts and the relevant scope definitions, then check whether the 2018 predecessor offers an age-cohort-relevant baseline (not a comparable time series without adjustment). If chart access fails, preserve the lead rather than guess values. In parallel as a research sequence, identify exact missing cross-tabs in platform reports. This is a source-led operation/scenario pilot, not an already calibrated insurer-allowed-cost model.

Any paid platform extract, shop outreach, many-model estimating exercise or large document review requires a separate proposal explaining fields, deliverable, expected cost/effort, alternatives and stop conditions. No financial forecast changed.
