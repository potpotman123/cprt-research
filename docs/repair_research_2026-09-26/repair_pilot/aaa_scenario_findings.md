## Audit
F-150 radar: detailed $1,292.16 versus summary $697.88; unresolved. Rear sensor summaries omit $22.50 included in detailed subtotals. Calibration shows 3.3 hours at $110 but $360 charged; potentially rounded hours. Preserve published totals; do not invent corrections or double-count components.

## Interpretation
A universal multiplier is not supported by these observations. A model-specific scenario is not a body-class treatment effect: brand, equipment, engineering, damage assumptions and included work can explain differences. Do not pool windshield/glass and collision scenarios without claim-category weights. Do not estimate real-world operation frequencies by counting the study scenarios. The front-cost decomposition remains quarantined because its published components conflict.

The useful model object is cost conditional on a defined repair scope. Aggregating it requires damage-scope frequencies for the appropriate vehicle cohort; using equal weights would be an assumption, not an estimate. New-vehicle 2023 costs cannot silently calibrate 7–12-year-old vehicles or prices in later periods. Estimating software may also encode included labor differently; required OEM steps, quoted work and allowed work must be separately tagged.

## Decision
Retain these as scenario stress tests and a specification for a stronger matched-work sample. Do not replace the earlier unvalidated 1% premium with an average of these ratios. Obtain a comparable older-vehicle scenario source before wider extrapolation. No revenue, TLF or valuation inputs changed.
